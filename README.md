
🎓 Tutorial Management System Analysis

📌 Project Overview

The Tutorial Management System Analysis is an Excel-based academy management and data analysis project developed for Success Gate Academy to organize student, staff, attendance, payment, and examination information, and transform tutorial center operations into useful business insights.

The project demonstrates practical skills in Microsoft Excel, data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables, data analysis, reporting, and dashboard development.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Manage student registration and staff records in a structured format.
- Track daily attendance across departments and dates.
- Record tutorial fees, examination fees, and payment status.
- Track staff salaries, allowances, and deductions.
- Reduce manual tracking of academy operations.
- Analyse student performance, attendance, and revenue.
- Provide useful reports for management decision-making.
- Present academy performance through clear summaries and a dashboard.

🗂️ System Components

The project includes:

- Student registration records
- Class timetable
- Daily attendance register
- Test and exam score records
- Staff records
- Staff salary records
- Tutorial fee payment records
- Examination fee payment records
- Summary and PivotTable reports
- Live dashboard

🔄 Academy Operations Process

The system follows a structured operations process:

Student Registration → Class Placement → Attendance Tracking → Fee Payment → Test/Exam Scoring → Examination Fee Payment → Report → Analysis

This structure helps ensure that student information can be entered, tracked, billed, assessed, and analysed efficiently from registration through to examination.

🧹 Data Cleaning & Validation

Data preparation was performed to improve the quality and consistency of the academy records.

Key activities include:

- Organising student, staff, and payment records into structured Excel Tables.
- Checking fields for consistency across Registration, Payment, and Examination Fees sheets.
- Applying appropriate data formats (dates, currency, phone numbers).
- Using data validation for fields such as Attendance codes (P/A/E/U/T) and Payment Status.
- Ensuring fee and score fields are suitable for formula-driven calculations.
- Using formulas to reduce manual billing and grading errors.

📊 Academy Operations Analysis

The analysis focuses on important operational measures such as:

- Total students by department (Science, Art, Commercial) and gender
- Attendance totals (Present, Absent, Excused, Unexcused, Tardy)
- Total tutorial fees, amount paid, and outstanding balance
- Total examination fees, amount paid, and outstanding balance
- Test and exam scores, grades, and remarks by subject
- Total staff, salary, allowances, and deductions
- Payment method and registration status breakdown

These measures help management understand student performance, attendance patterns, and revenue collection.

📈 Dashboard & Reporting

The academy dashboard/reporting section is designed to provide a quick overview of academy performance.

It can be used to monitor:

- Student count by department and gender
- Attendance trends
- Tutorial and examination fee collections and outstanding balances
- Staff count and payroll costs
- Student performance by subject and exam type (JAMB/WAEC)
- Payment method breakdown

PivotTables, charts, and summaries make the information easier to understand and explore.

💡 Business Value

The system can help a tutorial center:

- Improve student and staff record management.
- Reduce repetitive manual attendance, billing, and grading work.
- Monitor fee collection and outstanding balances.
- Identify at-risk students through attendance and performance trends.
- Track staff payroll costs by department.
- Produce clearer operational reports.
- Support data-driven academic and administrative decisions.
